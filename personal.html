Yours, privately

It asks before it acts.

An email is read back to you once, with who it goes to and the gist, and sent on your yes. A draft stays a draft until you say send. Anything aimed at someone else waits on your word.

You connect your mail and your diary yourself, and disconnect them whenever you like. Your own things never open for a customer or a caller: that is a rule in the code, not a setting.

What it will not do

Send in your name without a yes. Spend your money. Tell a caller what is in your diary or your inbox.
