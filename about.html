About
deputable /dɛpˈjuːtəbl/ adjective able to be safely deputed; fit to be delegated.

We started with one question: which parts of a working day can you honestly hand to an AI, and which parts should never leave a person's hands? The company is named after the answer.

The line

Some work is deputable. Some never will be.

A good deputy takes the routine off your desk and brings you the decisions. That's the standard we hold ours to. It answers the phone at seven in the evening, takes the details down properly, drafts the estimate, chases the paperwork. Then it stops, because the next step belongs to a person.

Deputable
  • Answering calls out of hours and on a busy line
  • Taking messages and writing up every call
  • Keeping track of every call back you owe
  • Drafting estimates, replies and invoices
  • Reading and sorting the photos and PDFs you send
  • Reminders, diaries and the mail that can't wait
Not deputable
  • Pricing and quote negotiations
  • Managing human resources
  • Escalated complaints and disputes
  • Contractual commitments
  • Decisions that carry your reputation

Our job is to empower and enable people, never to replace them. If a proposal of ours would quietly erase someone's judgment from a process, it's a bad proposal, and we'll tell you so.

How we work

Every business runs differently, so a deputy is set up around yours: your hours, your people, your rules in your own words. We do the intake, draw the workflows with you, handle the privacy paperwork and keep reading what the calls show. Your staff give approvals from their phones.

Most of our thinking is shaped by service businesses that live and die by the phone, such as garages, trades, property and logistics, but nothing about the approach is locked to an industry.

Our Mission

Remove the operational friction from growing businesses. When AI manages the repetitive calls, enquiries and system updates, your team responds faster and spends its day on the work that actually creates value.
